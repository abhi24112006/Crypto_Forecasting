--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. LIMITATIONS & ARCHITECTURAL COMPARISON NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Architecture & Capacity: The saved N-BEATS checkpoint is a 4-block, 4-layer MLP network (128 hidden)
   specialized purely on univariate Close sequences. TFT is a complex multi-modal attention architecture
   incorporating 13 exogenous technical indicators, which exhibited signs of overfitting / lag tracking
   during the volatile 2021-2022 regime.
2. Date Alignment Nuance: In the original final_tft.py script, predictions were indexed with the forecast
   origin date (row date) while predicting the pre-shifted 'Target' column (next-day Close). In contrast,
   nbeats_model.py labeled sequences with the target date. When aligned strictly to the exact calendar target
   day (N=451), both models evaluate on identical BTC market prices, confirming N-BEATS superiority.
3. Non-Stationarity & MASE Denominator: Because BTC traded at ~$700-$20,000 during the training period
   (2013-2020) and surged to $20,000-$68,000 in the test period (2021-2022), the in-sample mean absolute difference
   (131.12 USD) is naturally much smaller than the test-period daily swings (1423.26 USD), scaling up the MASE.
